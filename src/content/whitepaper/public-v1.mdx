# The C-Index: Securing the Digital Common

**Abstract**

* **The Diagnostic:** Technological leverage and capital enclosure have decoupled productivity from human labor compensation, accelerating platform rent extraction while eroding the traditional tax base.
* **The Failure Mode:** Historical wealth taxes and bureaucratic welfare models fail due to high deadweight loss, capital flight, administrative overhead, and misaligned economic incentives.
* **The Protocol:** The Citizen Index (C-Index) provides an automated, market-native mechanism that programmatically channels digital monopoly rents into a universal citizen dividend. By integrating real-time inflation throttling and autonomous sovereign debt annihilation, it preserves market pricing and enterprise autonomy while securing an enduring baseline and funding the transition to a post-scarcity, steady-state economy.

## I. The Modern Georgist Problem

In 1879, political economist Henry George published *Progress and Poverty*, identifying a fundamental paradox of the industrial age: as society advanced and infrastructure grew, poverty deepened rather than alleviated. George’s diagnostic was razor-sharp: the value created by the collective community was being privately captured and hoarded by those who held a monopoly over a finite, vital resource: Land. His remedy, the Land Value Tax, sought to return that "unearned increment" to the public without destroying the free-market incentives that drive human innovation.

150 years later, the economic landscape has fundamentally shifted. Land is no longer the primary engine of value generation. Capital equity, specifically in the form of the global, cross-border corporation, is the new land.

The value of modern mega-corporations is not derived solely from their internal machinery; it is built on the network effects, data generation, and active participation of billions of global consumers. Yet, the financial yield of this modern digital common is funneled into an increasingly concentrated pool of private ownership. The Citizen Index (C-Index) is the logical, technological extension of George’s vision. It ensures that the compounding value created by our global society is systematically returned to the individuals who make that value possible, codified legally not as asset confiscation, but as **Digital Ground Rent**—fair compensation for harvesting the sovereign digital commons.

## II. The Diagnostic: The Stagnation of Capital

The central flaw of 21st-century capitalism is not that it creates wealth, but that it systematically locks that wealth away in a permanent state of stagnation. In a healthy economic ecosystem, money is like water: it must circulate to sustain life. However, the modern global corporation is designed to act as a massive, automated gravity well, pulling capital inward and holding it indefinitely within a closed loop of private equity and <Tooltip text="The practice where massive financial entities (like private equity firms or index funds) continuously accumulate stocks and assets, keeping the profits locked within the financial elite rather than distributing them widely.">institutional hoarding.</Tooltip>

When a handful of mega-corporations and private equity firms absorb the vast majority of the world’s financial yield, they create an artificial economic drought for the rest of humanity. This is the <Tooltip text="A condition where massive amounts of wealth accumulate in a few hands (like corporate treasuries or billionaire trusts) and stop circulating in the general economy, starving everyday citizens of purchasing power.">Stagnation of Capital</Tooltip>.

Historically, reformers have attempted to break this stagnation by launching frontal assaults on the corporations themselves. These efforts are mathematically destined to fail because they immediately trigger the corporate "immune system." The C-Index applies a strict **Single-File Mandate**: it completely decouples operational governance from financial yield. By leaving the steering wheel firmly in the hands of the corporate founders, we render the corporate immune system blind to our presence. We do not want to run the company; we simply require a fractional, continuous return of its value to the global commons that fuels it.

## III. The Mechanism: The 1% Kinetic Dilution

The operational execution of the Citizen Index relies entirely on the leverage of market access, applied through a non-violent, mathematical mechanism known as <Tooltip text="The process of systematically reducing the percentage of a company held by its original owners by legally mandating the creation of new public shares, using market access as the driving force (kinetic energy).">Kinetic Dilution</Tooltip>.

The mechanism is simple: To retain the legal right to operate and sell goods or services within the European single market, any corporation exceeding a specific Dynamic Economic Threshold (e.g., top 1% of global market cap by volume) must issue new, non-voting capital stock equivalent to 1% of its total equity value annually to the Sovereign Citizen Trust.

Because this is a voluntary market-access toll rather than a domestic tax, it bypasses traditional constitutional limits. Furthermore, it preempts Article 17 expropriation challenges under the EU Charter; corporations are not having assets seized, they are contractually agreeing to a Sovereign Licensing Fee in exchange for access to a highly lucrative, enclosed economic zone.

Because the 1% dilution rate applies only to the remaining private equity balance each year, the decay follows a strict law of <Tooltip text="A process where a quantity decreases at a rate proportional to its current value. Because the 1% C-Index bite is taken out of a shrinking private pool every year, the amount taken gets smaller over time, meaning private owners are never entirely wiped out.">Exponential Decay</Tooltip>. After Year 1, private owners retain 99%. By Year 100, the private pool stabilizes at approximately 36.6%, leaving the Citizen Index with a commanding 63.4% equity stake in the world's largest corporate entities.

Faced with the choice between losing 100% of the European market today or accepting a fractional, 1% non-voting equity dilution over a century, the corporate calculators will choose compliance every single time. 

## IV. The Thermodynamic Loop: Day-One Dividends & The DON Throttle

The C-Index discards arbitrary bureaucratic delays in favor of immediate, algorithmic macroeconomic balancing. 

**1. Day-One Dividends**
Upon implementation, the Sovereign Trust immediately begins liquidating its equity yield, distributing fiat directly into citizen wallets. This provides an instant, unassailable baseline of survival, radically accelerating the fiat velocity of the local economy.

**2. The DON Throttle (Decentralized Oracle Network)**
A universal dividend introduces the risk of demand-pull inflation if purchasing power outstrips a nation's physical capacity to produce goods ($Q_{\text{max}}$). The C-Index neutralizes this via the DON Throttle. Smart contracts continuously ingest live supply-chain and capacity telemetry. If aggregate demand threatens to exceed real output, the DON algorithmically throttles the citizen dividend payout to cool the economy.

**3. Permanent Autonomous Debt Annihilation**
Crucially, the excess fiat throttled by the DON is **never** stored in a centralized sovereign vault, which would create a honey-pot for political capture. Instead, it is automatically routed to purchase and permanently annihilate sovereign debt bonds (e.g., NextGenerationEU obligations). This drains excess liquidity from the economy, perfectly controlling inflation while continuously erasing government liabilities.
